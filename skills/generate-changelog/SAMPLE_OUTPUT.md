## Notes on the sample

- Conventional prefixes (`feat:`, `fix:`, `refactor:`, `docs:`, `chore:`,
  `remove:`) are matched first; the prefix is stripped from the bullet.
- Non-conventional messages (`Fix crash...`, `Add retry...`, `Revert "..."`)
  are caught by the keyword fallback and land in the correct section.
- `Revert "..."` → **Removed** (keyword `revert`).
- Re-running prepends a new dated section, preserving prior releases.
